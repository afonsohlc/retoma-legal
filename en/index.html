Retoma

routine without guilt

For anyone who started a routine, missed a day and dropped everything. Retoma doesn’t count streaks: it measures how long it takes you to come back.

In development for iPhone and Apple Watch

  • Tomorrow’s workout starts tonightYou set the workout time and the app plans the evening backwards: when to finish dinner, when to put screens away and when to sleep.
  • Nothing to logWorkouts, sleep and mood come from the Health app and your Apple Watch. Your watch proves it.
  • What counts is coming backA hard day counts as rest, and after a day that didn’t work out the app shows how to restart, not what you lost.
  • Food without counting caloriesDid you follow your nutritionist’s plan? One tap a day.
  • Your data is yoursNo account, no server, no ads. Everything stays on your iPhone, backed up to your own iCloud.
